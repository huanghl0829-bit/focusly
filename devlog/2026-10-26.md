## Blockers / notes
- The live site is a separate origin from local dev (127.0.0.1) and from any future custom domain — browser data does NOT travel between them; use Settings → Export/Import backup to move a workspace.
- Updating the live site: copy the changed files to /tmp/focusly-site-build and push (or re-run the same flow); bump `?v=` + `CACHE` in sw.js so clients refresh.
- "Again" (10 min) does not requeue within the same session — a card rated Again simply comes due again in 10 minutes on the schedule. Fine for now; Anki-style same-session relearning could come later.
